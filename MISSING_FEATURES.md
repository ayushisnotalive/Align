# Missing Dating App Features

Here is a comprehensive list of features found in top-tier dating apps (Tinder, Bumble, Hinge) that are not yet implemented in our app:

## 1. Monetization & Premium Tiers 💰
- **"Who Likes You" Grid:** Allowing users to see who swiped right on them before they swipe.
- **Super Likes / Roses:** A special, limited action to stand out to a potential match.
- **Boost / Spotlight:** Paying to have the profile shown to more users for 30 minutes.
- **Rewind / Undo:** The ability to undo a left-swipe.
- **Read Receipts:** Knowing if a match has read your message.
- **Advanced Filters:** Filtering the discover feed by height, education, or lifestyle choices (usually locked behind a paywall).

## 2. Rich Chat & Media 💬
- **Voice Notes in Chat:** Sending audio recordings in messages.
- **In-App Video & Audio Calling:** Calling matches without giving out a real phone number.
- **Media Sharing:** Sending GIFs, stickers, or photos directly in the chat.
- **Reactions:** Double-tapping to "heart" or react to a specific message.

## 3. Engagement & Gamification 🎮
- **Specific Prompt Interactions:** Like Hinge, allowing users to "like" or "comment" on a specific photo or prompt answer to start a conversation, rather than just swiping on the whole profile.
- **Daily Curated Picks ("Standouts"):** A curated list of 5-10 highly compatible users updated daily.
- **Speed Dating / Blind Date Mode:** Timed, text-only chat events where users talk before seeing each other's photos.
- **Push Notifications:** Native push notifications (APNs/FCM) for new matches, messages, and profile likes.

## 4. Safety & Moderation 🛡️
*(Note: Some of this is planned for our upcoming Phase 8: Safety & Admin)*
- **In-App Reporting & Blocking:** Detailed reporting flows for harassment or fake profiles.
- **Automated AI Moderation:** Automatically detecting and blurring inappropriate photos or flagging toxic messages.
- **Selfie Video Verification:** AI-driven face-matching against profile photos to earn a "Blue Tick".
- **Incognito Mode:** Hiding the profile from everyone except people the user has explicitly swiped right on.

## 5. Machine Learning & Algorithm 🧠
- **Elo / Desirability Scoring:** Dynamic ranking of profiles to show equally engaged users to each other.
- **Collaborative Filtering:** Showing users similar profiles based on what their "type" seems to be (based on historical swipe data).

## 6. Live API Integrations 🔌
- **Real Spotify Integration:** Currently we have the database field, but we need the OAuth flow to pull in their actual "Top Artists".
- **Real Instagram Integration:** Fetching the latest 6 photos from a user's IG grid via the Instagram Graph API.
